St. Aurora General Hospital — Services

Comprehensive care across departments — quality, safety, and compassion

Below is an overview of the hospital's primary services, how they operate, and what patients can expect when visiting each department.

Emergency & Trauma

24/7 emergency department providing rapid triage, stabilization, and coordination with specialty teams for trauma, acute medical care, and urgent surgical needs.

  • Rapid triage and resuscitation
  • Dedicated trauma team and operating theatre access
  • Advanced imaging on-site (CT, X-ray)

Outpatient Services (OPD)

Specialist consultations across cardiology, neurology, dermatology, ENT, ophthalmology, orthopedics, pediatrics and more. Appointments, walk-ins, and follow-ups are supported.

  • Specialist clinics and diagnostic workups
  • Minor procedures and day-care treatments
  • Medication reconciliation and discharge planning

Inpatient Care (IPD)

Private and shared rooms, multidisciplinary care teams, and care coordination for patients requiring admission for medical or surgical treatment.

  • 24/7 nursing and monitoring
  • Care plans and family communication
  • Rehabilitation and therapy referrals

Surgery & Intensive Care

Operating theatres for elective and emergency surgeries, staffed by experienced surgeons and anesthetists. Intensive Care Units provide advanced monitoring and life support.

  • Pre-op assessment and surgical planning
  • Post-op critical care and pain management
  • Infection prevention & safe surgical checklists

Radiology & Laboratory

Comprehensive diagnostics including X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI (if available) and a full laboratory with rapid turnaround for critical tests.

  • Timely diagnostics for faster treatment decisions
  • Outpatient imaging and inpatient requests
  • Quality-controlled laboratory testing

Pharmacy

On-site pharmacy provides inpatient and outpatient prescriptions, counselling on medicines, and support for affordable therapeutic options.

  • Prescription dispensing and counselling
  • Support for chronic medication refills
  • Drug interaction checks and safety counselling

Rehabilitation & Allied Health

Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and other allied services to support recovery and functional independence after injury or surgery.

  • Individualised rehab plans
  • Outpatient therapy and home exercise programs
  • Coordination with discharge planning

Patient Experience & Feedback

We welcome feedback from patients and families. Use the 'Patient Feedback' button on the main site to submit comments about staff behaviour, wait times, cleanliness, or any other aspect of care. Feedback helps drive improvements and informs our quality programs.
